01

The argument

RNA 3D prediction sits near TM-score 0.55 — the threshold for recovering roughly the right fold — while protein prediction routinely exceeds 0.9. The most informative result in the recent literature says the gap is not a scale problem.

What ERNIE-RNA actually does

One additive bias on attention logits

Aij = QiKjᵀ/√d + Bpair(i,j)

Bpair is set by hand: 3 for C–G, 2 for A–U, 0.8 for G–U, 0 on the diagonal. That is the whole advantage — and it nearly doubles cross-family F₁ against a model 7.5× its size, because a physical prior holds across families where learned statistics do not.

Thesis

If a crude three-valued prior outperforms 7.5× the parameters, then the marginal return on better physics exceeds the marginal return on more scale. PHAROS replaces that hand-set prior with a learned, ion-aware, motif-aware one.

02

Measured evidence

Three findings measured for this report on 180 BGSU non-redundant representative structures at ≤3.0 Å. Each one forces a specific architectural decision.

Finding 1 — RNA contact maps scale O(L), not O(L²)

117 chains · contact = any heavy-atom pair ≤ 8 Å, |i−j| ≥ 4

Forces

A dense L×L pair track would need 309 GB of activations at L=2048 and would spend over 99% of its compute on empty space. Sparsity is the ground truth, not an approximation. But how you exploit it turns out to matter just as much — a flat top-K selection fails on exactly the long chains where it is needed. That measurement, and the design it forced, are in §07.

Finding 2 — Ion coordination and rigidity are one phenomenon

24,623 nucleotides · 15 X-ray structures containing Mg²⁺ · B-factor z-normalised within each structure · cryo-EM excluded (ADPs not comparable) and a ≥30-RNA-residue guard applied

Forces

Ion effects and motif rigidity are not independent features. Predicting Mg²⁺ sites yields a rigidity map nearly for free and vice versa, so they share one physics expert rather than two disconnected heads.

The obvious confound, tested. Mg²⁺ proximity and dense packing both mark folded cores, so the gradient might merely restate that cores are ordered. Controlling for density reduces the association only from +0.420 to +0.372 (partial correlation) — the signal is largely independent of packing. A within-structure estimate gives +1.514 σ, positive in 4/4 structures holding both groups, bootstrap 95% CI [+1.256, +1.661]. Remaining caveats: B-factor absorbs resolution and refinement choices; the closest bin holds 319 nucleotides; the within-structure estimate rests on 4 structures. Association, not demonstrated causation.

Which ion to model

Cation inventory across 179 parsed structures

83% of direct Mg²⁺ coordination is to the non-bridging phosphate oxygens OP1/OP2; the next class is the major-groove guanine site G(O6)/G(N7). Coordination is mostly water-mediated: 0.77 inner-sphere vs 7.00 outer-sphere contacts per ion.

Negligible. A GNRA sequence is a GNRA tetraloop only when it caps a hairpin, so linear k-mer matching mostly counts helical positions. Motif identity requires the interaction graph. Forces

Coevolution is not a weak feature to blend in at fixed weight — for some families it is very nearly sufficient alone, for others close to useless. That spread (0.333 to 1.000) is itself the argument for conditional rather than fixed-weight use. Neff/L is a defensible router feature: computable before the model runs, and the literature is unambiguous that shallow RNA MSAs degrade coupling analysis (the stated motivation for rMSA, RNAcmap3, RNA-MSM).

But our own depth split is weak evidence, and the earlier version of this page overstated it. Exact permutation p = 0.0455 on a post-hoc threshold; Spearman(Neff/L, precision) = +0.224 over n=12; the deep arm has n=2; and a shallow family (THF riboswitch, Neff/L = 0.279) reaches precision 1.000. The architectural case rests on the literature and on the between-family variance, not on this split. Further caveats: ground truth is Rfam consensus secondary structure, not 3D contacts; the parser omits WUSS pseudoknot brackets (53 of 537 pairs, 9.87%), which deflates precision; APC-MI is a simplification of full DCA. All biases push the number down, so 0.670 is a lower bound.

A′

Physics-biased attention

Aij = QiKjᵀ/√d + Bwc + Belec(cion) + Bmotif + A(l−1)

Belec = −λ q²eff ℓB exp(−κdij)/dij, with Manning condensed fraction θ = 1 − 1/(zξ) and Debye κ from ionic strength. Only λ is learned. b is the axial charge spacing, not the P–P contour distance — for A-form RNA, 2.8 Å per base pair carrying 2 charges gives b = 1.40 Å, ξ = 5.11 and θ = 0.804 (qeff = −0.196). The often-quoted 0.76 is the B-DNA figure. This is how salt reaches the logits.

Mixture-of-experts feed-forward

32 fine-grained routed experts (dff=512) + 2 always-on shared, top-4 → 6 active of 34. Watson–Crick helix lives in a shared expert so the routed 32 never relearn it. Balancing is auxiliary-loss-free — 5S rRNA alone is 5,976 BGSU clusters, so a balance loss would fight the biology. 06

What could sink it

R1

Block-detection recall. Superseded but not eliminated — the flat design was measured at ~20% recall and replaced (§07). The hierarchical track has a 100% recall ceiling by construction, but whether a trained model attains it is unproven. Recall must be reported per length bin and per level.

R2

Ionic metadata — audited, partially confirmed (§08). ~36% of structures yield a recoverable ionic condition. Worse, recorded Mg²⁺ spans only 5–15 mM because the PDB is survivorship-biased. The learned ionic response must come from RMDB titration series, not the PDB. The closed-form physics term and the Mg²⁺ site head are unaffected.

R3

Crystallographic ions are not the solution ensemble. Resolved Mg²⁺ are site-bound only; the diffuse Manning atmosphere that neutralises ~80% of backbone charge (A-form RNA, θ = 0.804) is invisible to crystallography. The explicit head learns site binding, not the atmosphere.

R4

B-factor is a noisy rigidity proxy, absorbing resolution, occupancy and refinement choices. Excluding non-comparable cryo-EM ADPs left only 31 of 137 parsed structures usable.

R5

The chemical-probing gap. Ribonanza's 2.1M DMS/SHAPE-probed sequences — ~315× more supervised examples than the 6,661 with 3D — are not in the catalogue. Highest-priority acquisition.

R6

Evaluation honesty. Any improvement claim must be made on blind sets (CASP15/16, RNA-Puzzles), never on rRNA-saturated random splits. Sequence-identity deduplication is mandatory.

07

What validation changed

The efficiency claim was the load-bearing one, so it was tested rather than assumed. It did not survive. This section records the failure and the design it forced — the most useful result of the session.

The flat top-K design collapses on long chains

Recall of true 3D contacts in the top K = 32L, sequence-only proposer

Discarded

The headline mean of 0.795 is almost entirely an artefact of short chains, where K = 32L already covers 59% of the map — a random scorer reaches 0.746. Where sparsity actually matters, the flat design recovers one contact in five. A banded fallback fails too: median contact separation is 78 nt on long chains, and even a ±512 band misses 19%.

And deciding whether a 16×16 block holds any contact aggregates 256 pair-decisions into one — the model ranks 32k blocks, never 4.1M pairs.

Confirmed

Two things arithmetic alone could not settle. The dense baseline genuinely does not run — 12.9 GB of activations at L=2048 and 51.5 GB at L=4096, on a 14 GB machine. And at the largest length both can run, the hierarchical track is ~83× faster (0.10 s vs 8.26 s) with peak memory below measurement noise against the baseline's 5.15 GB. Cost falls monotonically with length (9.2% → 0.96%) because the budget grows as c·L while the dense map grows as L² — it gets cheaper relative to dense exactly where it is needed.

BUGS

Two silent defects found while building it, both of which would have degraded a trained model rather than crashing. The L3 expansion paired block rows with columns element-wise, materialising only each block's diagonal — 4 pairs per 4×4 block instead of 16. And the L2 budget, expressed as a fraction of all pairs, was clamped by masking to a quarter of target. Both pinned effective c at 5.0 while the code appeared to work. Budgets are now stated in the same units as the measurement (K = c·L).

OPEN

What this does not prove. The measurements show the information is present and the cost affordable. They do not show that a trained model will find the occupied blocks — that needs training. The nearest external evidence is ERNIE-RNA's zero-shot Top-L/1 contact precision of 0.68 from attention alone, which suggests learned representations rank contacts far better than the sequence-only heuristic tested here.

Survivorship bias

Mg²⁺ spans only 5–15 mM, and this is not a sampling accident. Nobody deposits a structure of unfolded RNA. Every PDB entry was solved under conditions chosen because the RNA folds there, so the archive holds almost no contrast between folding and non-folding ionic regimes. A model trained on deposited structures alone would see ionic condition as near-constant and correctly learn to ignore it.

CONSEQUENCE — Training stage 5 as originally specified is not viable on PDB data alone. It is rewritten to condition on RMDB Mg²⁺ titration series (SHAPE/DMS across an Mg²⁺ ladder on the same sequence), with deposited structures supplying only site-level supervision. This promotes chemical-probing acquisition from "most valuable missing asset" to prerequisite for the headline claim — and note that Ribonanza alone does not supply it; the titration series in RMDB do.
