# Audit, 2026-09-24/25

One night of hunting for one class of defect: **a component that exists, is
measured, and whose output is never checked for basic validity.** Sixteen
findings. Everything below is pinned in `scripts/sampling/verify_claims.py`
(`R1`–`R13`) or in a test, so none of it depends on this document.

The pattern was consistent enough to name: **almost every statistic this
codebase computed for diagnostic purposes was being computed and dropped at the
call site.** `r, _ = model.motifs(pair)`. `aux` keys discarded in the trunk.
Four CSV columns declared and never written. A shell command whose exit status
nobody read.

## What was wrong with the data pipeline

- A training pool is **0.54 of one length-sorted shard** — total-variation
  distance **0.342** from the corpus, pool mean 201 nt against 320. The
  docstring claimed the opposite. Fixed with `--interleave 8` → **0.112**.
- `--max-batch 512` bound on the sequence **count**, not the token budget:
  **92.5%** of batches capped on short shards, real tokens per step 83,899
  against 194,607. Fixed to 2048.

## The correction that matters most

**The held-out curve tracked all night measures one length band.** The legacy
sample is `eldors_c020_shard0004` alone: **52.7% of it is 20–79 nt against the
corpus's 6.5%**.

| step | legacy (c020) | corpus-weighted | vs the 2.0165-bit unigram |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9000 | 1.6495 | **1.8396** | 18.2% → **8.8%** |
| 9500 | 1.9527 | 1.9135 | 3.2% → 5.1% |

Scored per band, the +0.303-bit "collapse" over 9000→9500 is **+0.074**
corpus-weighted, with +0.3504 of it in 20–79 nt and two of five bands not
moving at all. `--split stratified` matches the corpus histogram and is what
the watcher now runs; `heldout_mlm.csv` is frozen at step 9,750.

## What passed

- **Coevolution is attached to the right chain.** Scored against the
  depositor's own annotation, independent of Rfam: precision@L/5 **0.6356**,
  recall@L **0.5237**, random baseline 0.0064 — **28.4× enrichment**.
- **The Watson-Crick gap is real**: 19 readings, all positive, mean **+0.0926**,
  3–4σ. But **flat** (−0.28σ over 4,750 steps) — the pairing signal was there
  by step 5,000 and has not grown.
- MoE balance shows no drift: per-1,000-step means 0.183 → 0.199 → 0.202 →
  0.200 → 0.195 → 0.203.
- The LR schedule is a function of tokens, not steps; Muon's LR follows the
  same cosine. MLM masking is 14.79% / 79.96% / 10 / 10 as specified.
- Every CSV this project writes is self-consistent (the two ragged BGSU files
  are third-party and natively ragged).

## Claims withdrawn

- "The batch size explains the step-8,500 regression." Step 8,750 ran on a
  **smaller** batch and recovered. The aggregate correlation survives
  (partial r −0.52, t −2.8); it does not license a per-point story.
- "At 8 loops the model is worse than the corpus unigram entropy." 2.0064
  against 2.0165 is 0.5% *better*. It retains 0.0101 of the 0.3533 bits it
  holds at 2 loops — **2.9%**.
- "18–20% better than base frequencies." Corpus-weighted it is **~8.8%** at
  step 9,000, and 4.7% on the first stratified reading at step 9,750.

## Open decisions

1. **Recycle depth.** The model works at exactly the depth it trained at:
   1.6632 bits at 2 loops, 1.9718 at 1, 1.9711 at 4, 2.0064 at 8. The config
   advertises 8 loops and 144 effective layers; training exercises 2 and 36.
   Stage 5 defaults `--sample-loops` **on** and would feed depths 1–8 to a
   trunk that collapses at everything but 2. Recommended:
   `--sample-loops --n-loops 4` — +12% time, 72 effective layers.
2. **Whether to restart stage 1.** Pool interleaving, `--max-batch 2048`, the
   per-corpus held-out split and GPU headroom for evaluation all take effect
   only on a restart. At ~4.7% better than unigram with 2.5B tokens left, a
   corrected data pipeline is worth more than finishing a run whose pools are
   single length bands.

## What is still not known

The rest of stage 1 is **not predictable from this curve**. A power law with a
floor and a log-linear fit describe the 24 clean readings about equally well
(rms 0.0536 vs 0.0579) and disagree at 4B tokens by 0.200 bits — more than half
the total improvement measured so far. The binding constraint is scatter, not
token range, which is why the pool and batch fixes matter beyond training.
